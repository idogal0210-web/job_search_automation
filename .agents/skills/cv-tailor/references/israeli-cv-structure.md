# Israeli CV Structure Reference for Engineering & Tech

## Header:
- Full Name (H1)
- City only (תל אביב / Tel Aviv) | Phone (from base profile) | Email | LinkedIn

## Professional Summary:
- 3-4 lines maximum. Written without "I" / "אני".
- Years of experience + domain core competencies + 1 standout metric.

## Work Experience (Reverse Chronological):
- Formula: `[Action Verb] + [What was done] + [Quantified result / metric]`
- 3-5 high-impact bullets per role.
- Hebrew action verbs: ניטרתי, הובלתי, ייעלתי, תיאמתי, בניתי, אוטמתתי, ניהלתי, חנכתי.
- English action verbs: Monitored, coordinated, spearheaded, streamlined, automated, managed, optimized.

## Military Service:
- IDF Unit, Role, Rank, Dates.
- Focus on leadership, technical systems, and non-classified operational skills.

## Education & Certifications:
- Degree / Diploma, Institution, Graduation year, Honors / Final Project.
