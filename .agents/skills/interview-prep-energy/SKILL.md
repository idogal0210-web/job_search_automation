## How to Conduct Interview Prep
When Ido requests interview prep for a specific role or company:

1. **Role Analysis:** Identify the top 5 technical and behavioral questions most likely to be asked.
2. **Behavioral Questions (STAR Method):**
   - **S**ituation (e.g. Unplanned operational pressure drop during night shift)
   - **T**ask (Maintain supply stability safely without interrupting power stations)
   - **A**ction (Independent judgment, real-time coordination with platform and INGL)
   - **R**esult (Deviations mitigated, zero downtime, full safety compliance)
3. **Technical Deep-Dives:** Review gas flow/pressure concepts, PV infrastructure, electrical compliance, and safety standards.
4. **Mock Interview Simulation:** Ask questions one by one, provide immediate feedback, and refine responses.
